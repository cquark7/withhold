# Community guidelines

Everyone is welcome to participate, regardless of background or experience.
Treat contributors and users with respect. Keep feedback specific to the work,
assume good intent, and explain disagreements with evidence.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory remarks, personal attacks, and sharing
someone else's private information are not acceptable. Keep reports and examples
free of credentials and other sensitive data.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, or limit
participation to protect the community. These expectations apply to issues,
pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

To report a conduct concern, open an issue requesting a private contact without
including sensitive details or identifying affected people. The maintainer will
arrange a private follow-up.
For software vulnerabilities, use [the security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
